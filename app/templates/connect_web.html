Copy your sign-in from music.apple.com

This app reads your library the way Apple's own web player does. It needs the media-user-token cookie your browser gets when you sign in. It stays on this server and is only used to read your playlists. It lasts a few months; when it runs out, paste a new one.

  1. On a computer, open music.apple.com and sign in.
  2. Open the developer tools: F12, or Cmd+Option+I on a Mac.
  3. Chrome or Edge: Application tab → Cookies → https://music.apple.com. Firefox: Storage tab → Cookies. Safari: Storage tab → Cookies.
  4. Find media-user-token, double-click its value, copy it, and paste it below.

Have an Apple Developer account? Set APPLE_TEAM_ID, APPLE_KEY_ID and APPLE_KEY_FILE instead, and this page becomes a one-click sign-in.
