Spotify only lets an app read your playlists after you create a free "developer app" on Spotify's site and give this app its Client ID. You need Spotify Premium, because Spotify only lets Premium accounts use developer apps. Each person who uses this app connects their own Spotify account to the one developer app you make here: add them under User Management in the dashboard (Spotify allows up to five).

1. Create a Spotify app

Open the Spotify developer dashboard

Sign in with your Spotify account, click Create app, and fill in the form:

Then accept Spotify's terms and save the app.

2. Copy the Client ID

On your new app's page, click Settings and copy the Client ID: 32 letters and numbers. You don't need the client secret.

3. Paste it here and connect

Spotify then asks you to approve access to your playlists. After you approve, you come back here to pick which playlists to add.

If something goes wrong
  • Spotify's dashboard says the redirect URI isn't secure. The address starts with http:// but isn't 127.0.0.1. Open this page at http://127.0.0.1:<port> on the computer that runs the app, and use the address it shows.
  • Spotify shows "INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid redirect URI". The address saved in your Spotify app doesn't match. In the dashboard, open your app, click Settings, then Edit, and set Redirect URIs to exactly {{ redirect }}.
  • Spotify shows "INVALID_CLIENT: Invalid client". The Client ID is wrong. Copy it again from your app's Settings, and make sure it isn't the client secret.
  • Spotify returns a 403 error, or says your account isn't registered for this app. The developer app belongs to a different Spotify account. Create your own app, or ask its owner to add your account under User Management.
