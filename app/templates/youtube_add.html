Playlist link

On YouTube or YouTube Music, open the playlist, click Share, and copy the link. The playlist has to be Public or Unlisted. The app doesn't sign in to YouTube, so it can't read private playlists, Liked videos, or Liked Music.

How YouTube playlists work
  • Each video's title is split into artist and song: "Artist - Song (Official Video)" becomes "Song" by "Artist". For YouTube Music's "Artist - Topic" uploads, the artist comes from the channel.
  • Songs are looked for in your music folder first, then on Soulseek, as for the other services.
  • With {{ youtube_fallback_label }} on in Settings, a song Soulseek doesn't have is downloaded from the playlist's own video.
  • Music videos often have an intro or an ending, so for those the app doesn't require a Soulseek file's length to match.
  • From the playlist's page, you can also copy it to Spotify or Apple Music.
